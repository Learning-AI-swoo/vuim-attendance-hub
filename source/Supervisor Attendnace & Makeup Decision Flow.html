Supervisor Attendance & Makeup Decision Flow

Virginia University of
Integrative Medicine

Purpose: Use this decision flow during active clinic operations to manage lateness, absences, unexpected no-shows, and approved makeup students consistently.

Left Lane: Regular Attendance

Start: Student assigned to shift

Did the student arrive on time?

YES
Mark Present
NO

Record exact arrival time in Populi comments

Was student 1–30 mins late?

YES
Mark Tardy
NO

Was student 30–44 mins late?

YES

Mark Absent

  • May remain only if permitted
  • No treatment credits earned
NO

Was student 45+ mins late?

YES

Mark Absent

  • Student may not participate
  • Student must make up shift

Escalation Alert

If absent past 30 mins, no notice given, and not on LCS: Notify DCE Office immediately.

Right Lane: Makeup Student

Start: Student arrives for makeup

Is student listed in the blue Makeup section of the LCS for today/shift?

NO

Do not allow participation

Direct to DCE Office

YES

Allow participation as approved makeup

Assign student to group based on operations
Student must remain for full shift & stay engaged
Credit earned ONLY via active intern role

Documentation:

  • Sign Clinical Diary at end of shift
  • Document "No Patients" if applicable

Post-Shift Processing

Original supervisor updates the attendance record only after completing these three steps:

Verifying the approved makeup on the LCS
Reviewing the signed Clinical Diary entry
Entering the makeup date in Populi comments

Quick Operational Reminders

Record exact arrival times consistently.
Supervisors verify makeups; they do not approve them.
Blue Makeup section is the source of truth for verification.
Clinical Diary MUST be signed every shift.
Unexpected absence past 30m without notice = Escalation.
Document "No Patients" whenever applicable.
