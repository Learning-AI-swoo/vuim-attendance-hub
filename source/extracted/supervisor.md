## §1 — Purpose and How to Use This Guide [#purpose]
Standardized operational procedures for managing student attendance and makeup shifts in the EAM clinic. Focuses on operational execution; for formal policy language, refer to Student and Supervisor Handbooks.

## §2 — Core Supervisor Responsibilities [#responsibilities]
Supervisors are the primary agents of clinic accountability:
- **Accurate Attendance Entry** [#resp-attendance] — Record exact arrival times for all students in Populi.
- **Verification** [#resp-verification] — Confirm makeup eligibility via the **Live Clinic Schedule (LCS)**.
- **Documentation** [#resp-documentation] — Sign Clinical Diary entries and workbooks accurately, including notations for shifts with no patients.
- **Operational Execution** [#resp-execution] — Manage group assignments; ensure treatment credits awarded only for active intern roles.

## §3 — Recording Tardiness and Absences [#recording]
Record **exact arrival time** in the Populi comment field for any student arriving after the designated start of the shift (**8:30 AM or 1:30 PM**).
- **1 to 30 minutes late** [#tardy-window] = Tardy
- **30 minutes or more late** [#absence-threshold] = Absence

## §4 — Handling 30 to 44 Minute Late Arrivals [#late-30-44]
- Mark **Absent** in Populi.
- **No treatment credits** for that shift.
- If operationally permitted by supervisor, the student may remain for the shift.

## §5 — Handling 45+ Minute Late Arrivals [#late-45-plus]
- Mark **Absent** in Populi.
- Student **must leave the clinic** and make up the shift.

## §6 — Supervisor Notification of Unexpected Absence [#escalation]
If a student is absent past the 30-minute mark, AND the absence was not already on the LCS, AND the student has not contacted the supervisor: **notify the DCE Office immediately**. Ensures timely follow-up, attendance review, and proper documentation of unreported absences.

## §7 — Makeup Approval vs. Makeup Verification [#approval-vs-verification]
Supervisors **do not approve** makeup shifts. Only the DCE Office (or designated clinical administration) has approval authority. Supervisor's role is to **verify** that a student has received official approval before allowing them to join the shift.

## §8 — How to Verify an Approved Makeup [#verify-approved]
The **LCS** is the authoritative operational source. Check the **blue Makeup section** for the specific date and shift.
- Supervisors do **not** have independent access to student Populi confirmation forms.
- If a student claims an LCS error, they may show their processed Populi response or View Form result, but should be directed to **contact the DCE Office** before participating.

## §9 — What to Do When a Makeup Student Arrives [#when-makeup-arrives]
1. **Verify** [#step-verify] — Confirm student is listed in LCS blue section.
2. **Coordinate** [#step-coordinate] — Coordinate with reception/front desk as needed.
3. **Assign** [#step-assign] — Place in a group. Priority to existing single-intern groups; otherwise, the designated **Makeup Group**.
4. **Manage** [#step-manage] — Ensure active participation in patient care or directed clinical learning.

## §10 — Hours, Treatment Credit, and Participation [#credit]
- **Attendance Hours** [#credit-hours] — Credited when student attends the full shift, remains actively engaged, documents properly in the Clinical Diary.
- **Treatment Credit** [#credit-treatment] — Awarded only for active participation in **Lead Intern** or **Assistant Intern** roles.
- **No-Patient Scenarios** [#credit-no-patients] — Student must remain engaged in directed clinical learning activities as assigned.
- **Oversight** [#credit-oversight] — Supervisors sign Clinical Diary at end of shift as usual. **DCE handles final processing of treatment-credit limits** during formal workbook review.

## §11 — Clinical Diary Documentation [#diary]
- **Signature Required** [#diary-signature] — Sign student's Clinical Diary at end of every shift to confirm attendance.
- **No Patients** [#diary-no-patients] — Write "No Patients" in the diary entry if none seen.

## §12 — Absence Resolution and Attendance Updates [#resolution]
To resolve an "Absent" status in Populi:
1. Student presents signed Clinical Diary entry from the makeup shift to the **original supervisor** (supervisor of the missed shift).
2. Original supervisor verifies makeup completion against the LCS.
3. Original supervisor updates status in Populi from **Absent → Excused**.
4. Supervisor **must enter the date the makeup was completed** in the Populi comment field.

## §13 — School Closures and Rare Exceptions [#exceptions]
- **Closures** [#closures] — Holidays, snow days, etc., must be made up. Do NOT count toward "one excused absence per course, per quarter" allowance.
- **Incomplete Grades** [#incomplete] — Rare exception for documented emergencies. Refer eligibility questions to DCE Office.

## §14 — Common Supervisor Errors and Wrong Assumptions [#errors]
- **Unauthorized Approval** [#err-unauthorized] — Verbally granting a makeup without DCE approval.
- **Inaccurate Entry** [#err-inaccurate] — Recording school closures or snow days as "Present" instead of "Absent."
- **Processing Errors** [#err-processing] — Marking a completed makeup as "Present" instead of "Excused" in the original record.
- **Missing Data** [#err-missing-data] — Marking shift "Excused" without entering makeup completion date in Populi comment field.
- **Missing Escalation** [#err-missing-escalation] — Failing to notify the DCE Office when a student is unexpectedly absent past the 30-minute mark without prior notice or LCS indication.

## §15 — Key Takeaways [#key-takeaways]
- Record exact arrival time for every student who is not on time.
- 30–44 min late = Absence + loss of treatment credits.
- 45+ min late = Absence + student must leave the clinic.
- LCS blue section is the only authoritative source for makeup verification.
- Supervisors **verify** — they do not approve.
