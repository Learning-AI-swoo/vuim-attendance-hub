Student Absence-to-Makeup Checklist

Use this checklist when you will miss, or have missed, a clinic shift and need to complete the makeup process correctly.

Step-by-Step Process

1
Know you will miss, or already missed, a clinic shift
2
Notify your supervisor and copy the DCE Office
3
Submit the Makeup Request Form in Populi
4
Do not assume you are approved
5
Wait for the request to be processed
6
Check the processed Populi response through View Form
7
Verify the approved makeup in the blue Makeup section of the Live Clinic Schedule
8
Attend only the specific approved makeup shift
9
Tell the supervisor you are there for an approved makeup
10
Remain for the full shift and stay actively engaged
11
Get your Clinical Diary signed at the end of the shift
12
If no patients were seen, write “No Patients” in the Clinical Diary
13
Show the signed Clinical Diary entry to your original supervisor

Key Attendance and Makeup Rules

  • 1 to 30 minutes late = Tardy
    30 to 44 minutes late = Absence
    45+ minutes late = May not participate and must make up the shift
  • Approval is not assumed. Only formally approved makeup shifts may be attended.
  • The Live Clinic Schedule is the authoritative operational source for approved makeups.
  • Clinical Diary signature is required every shift.

This checklist is a quick-reference tool. The Student Clinic Handbook and official VUIM policies remain the formal authority.
