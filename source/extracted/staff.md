# Staff Makeup Scheduling SOP — Extraction

**Source:** `Staff Makeup Scheduling SOP - Spring 2026.pdf`
**Effective:** Spring 2026 · Doc revision: 04.18.26-YC
**Audience:** VUIM clinic reception and administrative staff

---

## §1 — Purpose and Scope [#purpose]
Defines the administrative role of clinic reception and staff in the student makeup process. Ensures makeup scheduling occurs only for approved students; maintains clinic flow through proper verification and communication.

## §2 — Staff Role in the Makeup Workflow [#role]
Reception/admin staff support the clinical makeup process by:
- Verifying makeup approval via the **Live Clinic Schedule (LCS)**.
- Supporting operational scheduling **only after** makeup approval is verified on the LCS.
- Coordinating with Supervisors regarding patient arrival and operational needs.

Staff do **not** approve makeup requests, adjudicate attendance rules, or determine treatment credit eligibility.

## §3 — Using the LCS as the Staff-Facing Operational Source [#lcs]
The **LCS**, located in the Populi Links section, is the authoritative operational source of truth for staff.
- **The Blue Makeup Section** [#lcs-blue] — Check the designated blue section for the specific date and shift.
- **Verification** [#lcs-verification] — Name + specific makeup date listed → operational context to schedule patients or update roster as directed.
- **Absence of Entry** [#lcs-absence] — Not listed in the blue makeup section for that date → not approved for operational scheduling.

## §4 — Operational Scheduling After LCS Verification [#post-verification]
Staff may support roster updates or patient scheduling **only after** the student's approved makeup placement is verified on the LCS and under supervisor direction.

## §5 — What Staff Should Not Treat as Proof of Approval [#not-proof]
Never operationalize a makeup based on:
- **Verbal Claims** [#not-verbal] — Student stating they were told they could attend.
- **Email Chains or Informal Messages** [#not-email] — Messages that do not change the authoritative operational record.
- **Supervisor Permission** [#not-supervisor] — Supervisors manage the floor but do not grant the underlying administrative approval for a makeup.

## §6 — When Staff Should Defer to the Supervisor [#defer-supervisor]
Defer to the on-site Supervisor for all real-time clinic flow and instructional decisions:
- **Group Placement** [#defer-placement] — Which specific group the makeup intern will join.
- **Group Feasibility** [#defer-feasibility] — Whether a specific group can accept an additional intern or specific patient.
- **Intern Roles** [#defer-roles] — Whether a student is acting as lead or assistant.

## §7 — When Staff Should Refer Issues to the DCE [#refer-dce]
Refer unresolved approval/workflow issues to the DCE Office (**dce@vuim.edu**) when:
- Student believes they are approved, but their name is missing from the LCS.
- Operational discrepancy that cannot be resolved through LCS + normal supervisor coordination.

## §8 — What Staff Are Not Responsible For [#not-responsible]
Staff roles are strictly administrative. Staff are NOT responsible for:
- **Documentation Adjudication** [#not-docs] — Reviewing or signing Clinical Diaries / workbook materials.
- **Credit Enforcement** [#not-credit] — Determining if an intern earned treatment or observation credit.
- **Attendance Decisions** [#not-attendance] — Deciding if an absence should be excused or unexcused.

## §9 — Related Scheduling Escalation Note: Late Patients [#late-patients]
Patient punctuality rule (clinic flow + EAM educational standards):
- **15-Minute Rule** [#late-15-min] — If a patient arrives **15 or more minutes late**, staff must NOT independently assure the patient they will be seen.
- **Supervisor Confirmation** [#late-confirmation] — Confirm with the Supervisor whether the group can still accommodate.
- **Final Authority** [#late-authority] — Supervisor makes the final decision based on remaining shift time and intern workload.

## §10 — Key Takeaways [#key-takeaways]
- **LCS Verification:** If student not in LCS blue section for that date, do not operationalize the makeup.
- **Operational Scheduling Follows LCS:** Always check LCS first.
- **No Verbal Approvals:** Always require formal operational confirmation.
- **Consult Supervisors:** Defer for all late-patient and group-placement decisions.
- **Narrow Staff Role:** Verify, coordinate, escalate. Do not approve, adjudicate, or determine credit.

## §11 — Disclaimer [#disclaimer]
Operational guide for staff. Formal authority rests with the VUIM Student and Supervisor Handbooks.
