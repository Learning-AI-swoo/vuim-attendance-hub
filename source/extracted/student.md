## §1 — Purpose and How to Use This Guide [#purpose]
Operational instructions for managing attendance and makeup shifts. Translates handbook policy into actionable steps. Use to understand: punctuality responsibilities, how to request makeups, how to verify authorization to attend a shift.

## §2 — Core Attendance Expectations [#expectations]
- Clinical training requires **100% attendance**.
- Clinic shifts are professional commitments to patient care; reliability is a cornerstone of EAM practice.
- Interns must be present, prepared, and in correct attire for the **entire** assigned shift.

## §3 — Tardiness [#tardiness]
Supervisors must record exact arrival time in Populi for every shift.
- **1 to 30 minutes late** = Tardy. [#tardy-1-30]
- **First Tardy** [#first-tardy] — may be converted to an excused tardy if intern arrives on time for all remaining shifts in the quarter.
- **Second Tardy** [#second-tardy] — written warning + automatic forfeiture of treatment credits for that shift.
- **Cumulative Tardiness** [#cumulative-tardiness] — repeated tardiness may trigger further disciplinary action per Handbook.

## §4 — Absences [#absences]
Any arrival after 30 minutes, or a missed shift, is an Absence.
- **30 to 44 minutes late** [#absence-30-44] — Absence. May remain if supervisor permits, but **no treatment credits** for that shift.
- **45 minutes or more late** [#absence-45-plus] — Absence. **May not participate.** Must leave clinic and make up the shift.
- **Notification** [#absence-notification] — If absent without already-approved makeup authorization through the DCE Office, notify supervisor and copy DCE by email **before the start of the shift**.
- **Unexcused Absence** [#unexcused-absence] — Absence without prior notification AND without an approved makeup request. Treated as **patient abandonment**.
  - **First offense:** three-week suspension from clinic.
  - **Second offense:** suspension from clinic for the entire quarter.

## §5 — Makeup Requests [#makeup-requests]
All missed shifts (excused absences, emergencies, school closures) must be made up **within the same quarter**.
- **Submission** [#submission] — Submit the **Makeup Request Form** (Populi → Links) at least **one week** prior to the missed shift OR the intended makeup date, whichever is earlier.
- **The "First Absence" Rule** [#first-absence-rule] — Only the first excused absence **per course, per quarter** allows participation in a makeup shift with **full treatment credit eligibility**.
- **Multiple Absences** [#multiple-absences] — More than one excused absence per course per quarter forfeits treatment credits for those makeup shifts. Documentation/excuse does not override unless a formal institutional exception is granted.
- **School Closures** [#school-closures] — Must be made up. Do **not** count toward excused absence allowance.
- **Intensive Session** [#intensive-session] — **No makeup shifts permitted** during the Intensive session. Do not register for a clinical course if you anticipate missing shifts during this period.

## §6 — How Approval Actually Works [#approval]
**Approval is not assumed.** Submitting a request does not authorize attendance.
- **Authority** [#approval-authority] — Approval granted **only by the DCE Office**.
- **Capacity** [#approval-capacity] — Makeup spots typically limited to **two interns per shift**. If full on the LCS, request is denied.
- **No Independent Authorization** [#no-supervisor-approval] — Clinical supervisors **do not** have authority to approve makeup shifts.

## §7 — How to Verify a Makeup Was Approved [#verify-approval]
Confirm approval **before** arriving.
- **LCS Blue Section** [#lcs-blue] — The blue Makeup section of the **Live Clinic Schedule (LCS)** is the authoritative operational source. If your name + approved date are listed there, you are authorized.
- **Populi Confirmation Channel** [#populi-confirmation] — Automated Populi email confirmation once a request is processed. Click **View Form** within that confirmation to review details.

## §8 — What to Do on the Makeup Shift [#on-makeup-shift]
1. **Check-In** [#checkin] — Notify supervisor immediately upon arrival that you are there for an approved makeup.
2. **Assignment** [#assignment] — Supervisor places you into an existing single-intern group or a designated **Makeup Group**.
3. **Engagement** [#engagement] — Remain on-site and actively engaged for the **full shift**. If no patients, participate in supervisor-led clinical learning activities.

## §9 — Hours, Treatment Credit, and Participation [#credit]
- **Hours** [#credit-hours] — Credited when you attend the full shift, remain actively engaged as directed, and document properly in Clinical Diary.
- **Treatment Credit** [#treatment-credit] — Earned only through active participation in an intern role (**Lead** or **Assistant**).
- **No Patients** [#no-patients-credit] — If no patients seen, no treatment credits for that shift.

## §10 — Clinical Diary Documentation [#diary]
Primary shift-level record of attendance and activity.
- **Signatures** [#diary-signature] — Obtain a supervisor's signature at the end of **every** shift, including makeups.
- **"No Patients"** [#no-patients-note] — If no patients treated, write "No Patients" in the Clinical Diary. This ensures hour credit.
- **Resolution** [#absent-to-excused] — To change Absent → Excused, show the signed Clinical Diary entry from the makeup shift to your **original supervisor**.

## §11 — Incomplete Grade and Rare Exceptions [#incomplete]
- Rare exception for documented emergencies or school closures.
- **Eligibility** [#incomplete-eligibility] — Generally only available if **60% of the clinical course** has been completed.
- **Usage** [#incomplete-usage] — Not for normal scheduling or non-emergency absences.

## §12 — Common Mistakes and FAQs [#faq]
Each FAQ gets its own anchor.

- **The "I Sent the Form" Mistake** [#faq-sent-form] — Submitting ≠ approval. Wait for Populi "View Form" confirmation and verify name on the LCS before attending.
- **The "Supervisor Said Yes" Mistake** [#faq-supervisor-said-yes] — A supervisor cannot approve a makeup independently. Formal DCE approval required.
- **The "No Patients" Mistake** [#faq-no-patients-leaving] — Leaving early because no patients. Stay full shift to earn hour credit.
- **What if I know in advance I will miss a shift?** [#faq-advance-miss] — Submit Makeup Request Form via Populi at least one week before missed shift or requested makeup, whichever is earlier.
- **What if I had an emergency and missed a shift?** [#faq-emergency] — Notify supervisor + copy DCE ASAP. Submit Makeup Request Form with supporting documentation.
- **What if I am 10 or 25 minutes late?** [#faq-10-25-late] — Tardy. May participate. See §3.
- **What if I am 35 minutes late?** [#faq-35-late] — Absence. May remain if supervisor permits, no treatment credits.
- **What if I am 50 minutes late?** [#faq-50-late] — Absence. May not participate. Must make up the shift.
- **What if I already used my one excused absence for this course this quarter?** [#faq-used-absence] — Additional missed shifts must still be made up. Additional excused absences forfeit treatment credits and may carry further consequences.
- **What if my request was submitted but I do not yet see approval?** [#faq-no-approval-yet] — Approval is not assumed. Wait for processed Populi response and verify on LCS before attending.
- **What if my Populi response shows approval but I do not yet see it on the LCS?** [#faq-populi-not-lcs] — Contact DCE Office before attempting to attend. Do not rely on processed response alone.
- **What if I completed my makeup shift?** [#faq-completed-makeup] — Get Clinical Diary signed; show signed entry to original supervisor so Absent → Excused.
- **What if I attended a makeup and there were no patients?** [#faq-makeup-no-patients] — Stay engaged in clinical learning. Document "No Patients" + obtain supervisor signature.
- **What if school closed (weather/campus-wide)?** [#faq-school-closure] — Must still be made up; does not count toward excused absence allowance.
- **What if I think I may need an Incomplete?** [#faq-incomplete] — Rare. Only documented emergencies or school closures, generally only if ≥60% of course completed.
- **What if I am an observer, not an intern?** [#faq-observer] — Observers must still meet attendance requirements + make up missed shifts. Observation does not earn treatment credits.
- **What if I came to a shift without formal approval?** [#faq-no-approval-attended] — Doesn't count toward makeup completion. Underlying absence may be classified unexcused. See §4.

## §13 — Key Takeaways [#key-takeaways]
- 1–30 min = Tardy; second tardy = written warning + treatment credit forfeiture.
- 30–44 min = Absence (no treatment credit).
- 45+ min = Absence; may not participate; must make up.
- Unexcused absence = patient abandonment; first offense = three-week clinic suspension.
- DCE Office is the only approval authority; LCS Blue Section is the authoritative schedule.
- "One excused absence per course, per quarter" is the standard.
- Clinical Diary signatures required every shift, regardless of patient volume.

## §14 — Disclaimer [#disclaimer]
Student Clinic Handbook remains the formal authority. In conflict, the Handbook governs.
